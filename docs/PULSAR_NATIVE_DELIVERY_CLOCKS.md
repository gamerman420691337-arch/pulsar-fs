# Native source delivery clocks and absolute lifetime

The private Linux source launcher now measures request-send and readiness-receipt instants around the worker's own monotonic Ready sample. Validated decoded frames retain an immutable body plus full attempt/capture identity, pinned worker/FFmpeg identities, original signed rational source PTS, pixel identity, worker delivery elapsed time, parent receipt, complete clock probe and conservative delivery interval. The facts explicitly use `decoded_stdout_local_monotonic_equal_rate` as their age basis. They do not claim source exposure age, measured camera/player clock rates, source buffering, authenticated capture or physical-device authority.

For parent request-send A, readiness-receipt B, worker Ready elapsed sample S and later worker delivery elapsed D, equal-rate local clocks bound delivery at `[A + D - S, B + D - S]`. The readiness round trip is retained as uncertainty, at most one second. The parent keeps the full interval rather than selecting a midpoint, replacing delivery with consumer time or silently narrowing its uncertainty. Source PTS remains a separate exact rational coordinate. The interval must remain strictly before the parent's absolute end, and its earliest instant cannot contradict the actual parent packet receipt. Invalid, excessively uncertain, regressing, expired or contradictory clocks permanently retire the worker.

`NativeSourceFrame` has private immutable facts/body, no clone or deserialization constructor, and a process-local origin. Its read-only facts may be archived and revalidated, but deserialized facts cannot recreate a measured frame handle. `delivery_age_at` uses the earliest bound for conservative delivery age and refuses samples before packet receipt, in the future or at/after lifetime expiry. Native stdout delivery excludes unknown exposure, encoding and decoder/source buffering age. Those delays cannot be recovered by relabeling this value as physical capture age.

`LiveSourceWorker::start_in_lease` now accepts the caller's existing process-local origin and absolute lease end. It rejects future origins, expired/reversed ends or lifetimes above thirty seconds before snapshot/native launch. The effective deadline is the earlier of the existing absolute end and the manifest's launch-relative budget. Staging, readiness, producer operations and frame consumption cannot reset that end. The independent process/cgroup watchdog and producer handle use the same effective deadline. Fresh actor/revision/boot/lease checks are still the successor adapter's responsibility; an `Instant` or archived clock fact conveys no authority.

The same absolute-lifetime admission is shared with persistent inference. The existing authenticated automatic-inference actor now passes the actual capture entry's origin/end into `LiveInferenceWorker::start_in_lease`; native launch no longer starts a fresh full lease after actor publication or thread scheduling. Fresh retirement checks and lease cancellation remain in place. Native model lifetime, output acceptance and physical-device stopping remain distinct acknowledgements.

Four pure clock regressions enumerate possible Ready sample offsets, conservative ages, delayed readiness/consumption and invalid/overflow/expiry cases. Two parent regressions verify deadline preservation and immutable runtime delivery-age refusal. Two private protocol regressions verify archive scope/pins/bounds and reject hardware-exposure/qualification claims. Actual confined native receipts additionally retain the measured probe, per-frame bounds, conservative ages and exact pins; they exercise a lease already in progress, delayed consumption, streaming before producer EOF, chunked intake and independent idle expiry with a still-open producer. An already-expired lease refuses before launch. These are authored stream checks, not real camera/media/model/Handy/platform/human/release qualification.

The subsequent [authenticated encoded ingress adapter](PULSAR_AUTHENTICATED_ENCODED_INGRESS.md) now publishes immutable native frames into capture leases, retains the delivery age basis in capture/inference reports, enforces fresh input/frame authority and the actual entry origin/end, and retires disconnected sources without rearming. Direct camera/device acquisition and source continuity remain open; `native_acquisition` remains false and bounded encoded ingress is advertised separately. Training and sterile evaluation were unchanged.
