# Bounded larger image-motion support

Detector generation now preserves accepted local flow and adds bounded coarse proposals for rejected points in analysis views at least 256 pixels on each side. A proposal from either of two smaller scales must pass a full-resolution forward/backward and photometric check before it becomes supported flow. Coarse matches alone never produce stroke evidence.

The existing input/work limits remain. The default configured component displacement bound is 26 analysis-view pixels; the general checked bound cannot exceed 34. Region consistency still needs three accepted points and a two-thirds consensus within one pixel. The worker preserves its initial 16 target samples. When a view is at least 256 pixels on each side and fewer than three target samples are supported, it adds up to 48 distinct interior samples under the same checks. The total remains at most 64; the three-point admission and consistency requirement are unchanged. Receipts include the actual target sample count. Smaller views keep the existing local path. Generation receipts record the configured search levels, recovered points and displacement bound. These are image-space estimates, with inferred stroke and unavailable gaps, not physical camera pose or calibrated motion.

Detector receipt recipe v3 identifies this change; current detector generation uses v4 for the later [scoped association correction](PULSAR_RELATIVE_MOTION_EVIDENCE.md). Archived v2 detector receipts remain readable through the checked clock-custody path; other generation modes keep their existing recipe identities. No persisted settings or historical results are rewritten.

## Evidence

The original 18-source-pixel whole-image shift produced unavailable coverage and export refusal at 480 by 640, while the explicit small view compensated three-pixel movement correctly. This failure to recover larger supported image motion is retained before implementation. Numerical tests check recovery of a known 12 by minus 10 pixel translation, an independent reverse cycle, preservation of accepted local evidence, lack of texture, edges, invalid shapes, non-finite points, point-budget overflow and bad displacement bounds.

The final native campaign passes all six pairs for each of the 6- and 18-source-pixel steps. At the model-sized view, the last 18-pixel pair expands from 16 to 64 attempted target samples and retains 14 supported points; it passes the unchanged consistency and half-pixel fixture checks. The largest 18-pixel error across both views is 0.089158 analysis pixels. The 6-pixel campaign remains unchanged, with maximum error 0.044940. The 36-pixel campaign is PARTIAL: five pairs are supported, but one model-sized pair has inconsistent target support and standard export is refused. This does not justify loosening admission or claiming arbitrary-motion coverage. Supported camera-only exports have four actions at position 50.

Exact native results and subjects are recorded in `evidence/roadmap-2026-10-11/multiscale-flow/checkpoint.json`. The controlled public-image fixture provides known translation; it is not real-media target quality, arbitrary camera-motion coverage, stereo/VR physical geometry, performance, native GUI acceptance, Handy behavior or release qualification. No annotations or training labels were invented. The full roadmap remains PARTIAL.
