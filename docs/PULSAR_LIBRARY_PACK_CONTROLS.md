An absent owner capability, no selection or a mismatched digest refuses before batch preparation. Profile/runtime/model bytes receive the Engine’s existing fresh admission checks during preparation and each future job.

It performs no automatic import, approval or selection. Owner approval remains unqualified native-execution intent; recorded status is not a fresh asset or quality verification.
