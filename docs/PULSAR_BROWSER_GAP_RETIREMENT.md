# Confirmed browser gap-proposal retirement

After a gap proposal is explicitly reviewed/committed and its original acknowledgement
is confirmed, choose **Inspect or recover gap proposal intent**. Check **Retire the
confirmed gap proposal, preserving Engine history**, then press **Retire confirmed gap
proposal**. Retirement binds original proposal/candidate/commit IDs and acknowledged
revision, requires a stopped preview and verifies authenticated candidate/program and
current-project readback. Unapproved or unconfirmed commits refuse; reconcile the
original operation first.

The private handoff becomes inactive while compact history remains. No Engine candidate,
source, project, upload/commit receipt, recording or export is deleted. **Inspect retired
gap proposals** reads up to 64 receipts without Engine work. A later committed master
can prepare another explicit gap proposal. Repeating old retirement returns its original
receipt without clearing a newer active repair. Retired intents cannot be uploaded or
committed as the active selection.

Older journals remain readable without invented history or approval. New gap preparation
preserves a retired predecessor's history under the same workflow lock; malformed/foreign,
duplicate and over-bound history refuse with bytes preserved. Recording and gap journals
stay separate but share the same compact proposal-receipt shape. A repeated complete
record/repair/export workflow must explicitly retire its confirmed recording handoff,
gap handoff and export pointer independently. Recording-index capacity and history/file
reclamation remain separate open work.

These are metadata lifecycle controls. Authored repairs remain synthesized evidence;
useful-model/media accuracy, arbitrary-tab capture, physical Handy synchronization and
platform/human/release qualification remain open. Private storage is unsupported on
non-Unix platforms. Full roadmap PARTIAL.
