# Parent-owned confined source launch

Linux `LiveSourceWorker` now launches the private [encoded-source role](PULSAR_NATIVE_SOURCE_WORKER.md) with exact admitted worker/FFmpeg snapshots, aggregate worker/memory/storage reservation, read-only bindings, namespace and cgroup confinement and an independent ten-millisecond watchdog. The parent absolute deadline starts before snapshot staging. The subsequent [authenticated encoded ingress](PULSAR_AUTHENTICATED_ENCODED_INGRESS.md) now connects this primitive to capture leases. Camera acquisition/continuity remain open; bounded encoded ingress is advertised separately and `native_acquisition` remains false.

The only producer handle is transferred once and cannot be cloned or reacquired. Its nonempty chunks are at most 65,536 bytes and cannot exceed the manifest's total intake bound. Local chunk-bound refusal leaves the handle usable; partial or ambiguous pipe writes permanently retire it. `finish` records the exact successfully forwarded SHA-256 and count before closing stdin. Dropping an unfinished producer retires the worker. Callers must drive the producer concurrently with frame consumption: bounded OS pipes apply backpressure, and callers cannot assume a whole stream can be sent before output is read. No unbounded encoded queue or automatic retry is introduced.

The reader has one bounded pending packet and fixed 12,288-byte RGB bodies. Parent acceptance independently checks the complete attempt/capture identity, ready-before-frame ordering, ordinal starting at zero, strictly increasing exact signed rational source PTS, nonregressing worker delivery time, body size and actual pixel hash. Completion additionally requires the parent's sealed exact intake count/hash, exact accepted frame count, no appended output, terminal EOF and successful native exit. Frames received before an unsuccessful completion are streamed observations, not proof that the complete source succeeded. Every transport/sequence/native failure permanently retires that worker.

The watchdog cancels and reaps the owned process/cgroup even when nobody calls a transport method. Dropping the parent closes its receiver before joining the reader, kills/reaps the native group and joins its owned threads; a producer handle retained elsewhere cannot prevent parent cleanup. Reservations and private snapshots remain owned until parent drop. No source/model/device authority lock is held during native I/O. Parent process reap is distinct from a physical device stop acknowledgement.

The subsequent [native delivery clock adapter](PULSAR_NATIVE_DELIVERY_CLOCKS.md) now measures the worker/parent readiness bracket, retains immutable scoped delivery facts and conservative ages, and accepts an existing absolute lease origin/end. It does not erase source/decoder buffering or claim camera exposure age. The authenticated encoded adapter now propagates that delivery age basis and publishes frames under fresh lease authority. Continuous acquisition/renewal, real camera/player clocks, model quality, GPU six-axis, Handy transport/firmware/physical stopping and platform/human/release qualification remain open. Training weights and the negative sterile evaluation are unchanged.

Four parent sequence tests cover ordering, exact rational progression, delivery regression, corrupted/short bodies and completion mismatch. The compiled native fixture separately uses the actual parent confinement path with authored YUV4MPEG2 bytes. It requires an output frame before producer EOF, exercises larger intake across the 64-KiB chunk boundary, checks exact successful completion and reservation release, and refuses wrong tool bytes/declared format, frame/input excess, cancellation, dropped input and expiry while the producer remains open and the consumer is idle. Its receipt explicitly records no lease integration or physical dispatch. These authored checks do not qualify a real camera, model or Handy.

The final source-bound gate results and retained native receipts are recorded in [the validation checkpoint](evidence/roadmap-2026-10-01/parent-source-launch/validation.json).
