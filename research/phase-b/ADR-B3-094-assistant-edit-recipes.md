# ADR B3-094: approve fixed assistant recipes through authored candidates

Decision: offer two values-only recipes per nonempty motion axis: inversion and amplitude halving around the midpoint. Bind their IDs to project, revision, program digest, axis and operation. Capture explicit recipe focus in the local assistant context. The model chooses a checked ID; it supplies no coordinates, authority or executable code.

Apply a completed edit proposal only after separate approval of its context and proposal digests. Require Read and Edit authority and the original owner/project/revision/Engine lifetime. Reconcile through the existing values-only provenance kernel, preserve protected interpolation anchors and gaps, and publish an authored candidate, exact receipt and original request identity in one metadata transaction. The existing commit, automatic authored-motion protection and undo/redo controls remain authoritative.

Consequences: immutable prepared content can outlive a refused metadata transaction under the existing content-store policy. All-protected/no-change proposals are refused. The desktop keeps exact retry identity in memory; durable saved edit requests and restart resolution remain subsequent work. No model, media, native desktop, device or human acceptance is implied.
