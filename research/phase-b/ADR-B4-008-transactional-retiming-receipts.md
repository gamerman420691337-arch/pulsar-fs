# ADR B4-008: Retiming intent and result commit as structured facts

Status: implemented local authored receipt; export proof integrated in B4-009; portable closure open.

B4-007 introduced explicit retiming but its revision description alone cannot bind a machine-readable source-clock proof. Inferring selected axes or placement intent from prose would invent evidence. A new schema-6 table therefore records strict version-1 receipt bytes and their digest under the same transaction as the edit, revision/history and request acknowledgment. A failed receipt insert rolls back those authoritative records. Schema-5 history is preserved without fabricated receipts.

The receipt binds base/result revisions, recorded actor/session/request and fingerprint, retained placements, explicit axes and immutable before/after identities. Status checks the recorded lineage/actor/request and cached result, verifies placements/program identities, reserves memory, reads hash-verified historical motion and recomputes the complete transformation. Merely rehashing a forged receipt cannot bypass its independent recorded bindings. This is local authored evidence, not cryptographic nonrepudiation or external human identity verification.

The database receives an explicit schema boundary and startup validates its exact column, composite key and foreign-key layout. Current startup probes schema/layout read-only before writable SQLite initialization. The initial frozen-reader fixture exposed checkpointing of committed WAL pages: a main-file hash by itself did not represent the logical committed database. The historical fixture now uses a checkpointed quiescent subject; the current reader additionally refuses a future schema committed only in WAL while preserving both main/WAL hashes. This follows SQLite's [documented WAL and last-connection checkpoint behavior](https://www.sqlite.org/wal.html), not an assumption that closing or opening writable SQLite is byte-preserving.

The frozen schema-5 executable remains unchanged and is identified by its exact hash. Its refusal of a quiescent schema-6 subject and current reopening are native checks. [B4-010](ADR-B4-010-portable-retiming-facts.md) now retains original retiming facts in format-5 archives and recomputes them during import; cloned declarations cannot become destination-local authenticated proof. [B4-009](ADR-B4-009-retimed-source-clock-proof.md) now integrates verified single-clock retiming chains for explicit source-time Stash publication. Missing legacy receipts remain explicit NotFound, not reconstructed intent.

Protocol, layout, transactional rollback, bounded/corrupt status, attribution-forgery, replay/history/restart, historical refusal and current WAL-only future-schema cases are retained separately from real-media, physical-device, model, platform and release qualification.
