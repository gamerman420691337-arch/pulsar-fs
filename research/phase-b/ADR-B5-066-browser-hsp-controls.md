# Keep browser HSP controls explicit and natively owned

Expose the existing generated-stream preparation, Bind, Play, Stop and continuity
operations through a distinct browser HSP owner. Hosted HSSP Start selects a
complete script and has a different qualification and lifetime contract; reusing
its acceptance would misdescribe an analysis-produced stream. Reuse local HTTP
scope and cleanup conventions while retaining the existing Engine source, model,
device, settings and timing gates.

Bind receives a typed source snapshot captured by the native Preview owner. The
page supplies explicit choices and consent, not capture, inference, media or timing
identities, motion samples or replacement timestamps. This snapshot describes the
selected source; it cannot manufacture native delivery, fresh timing or acceptance.
The Engine independently checks the actual source and its unchanged reviewed
contracts before admitting the stream.

Keep mutation, inspection/status and Stop/cancellation in separate bounded native
lanes. A shared preview queue would let frame or editor work delay cleanup. Reserve
each original intent identity before submission and retain it through uncertainty;
late responses cannot retarget an operation or restore readiness. Only a queued
intent proven never submitted may settle locally as unsubmitted. An attempted or
uncertain operation cannot acquire that status from a timeout, missing history or
failed response. Status never renews a deadline or issues a replacement intent.

Video Play starts analysis and timing only. Synchronization consent, the selected
source environment acknowledgment, online control consent, device Play and bounded
continuity consent remain separate explicit choices. Source succession preserves
the original stream and Play; it does not trigger another Bind or Play. Natural
finite input retains the selected Engine closure/drain policy, while interruption
retires the selected lifetime through its original cleanup obligations.

Once Setup is bound and at least two points are acknowledged, ongoing Append
work or its pending metadata must not require an idle interval before the client
can reserve an explicit Play or continuity intent. The Engine already serializes
effects, retains the original Play reference and expiry, and rechecks native
source/clock authority. Periodic UI observations can otherwise repeatedly land on
Append work until the bounded prefill fills. This changes client readiness only;
retirement, Stop, insufficient prefill, uncertainty and existing original-intent
checks remain binding, and no effect is retried or retimed.

Successor preparation retains its report but does not open the next capture's
bulk writer. Only the validated original transfer reservation installs the F seed
and opens B ingress; the client then opens B's channel before uploading the original
G pair. The Engine ingress check, transfer identity and no-replacement upload latch
remain unchanged.

Predecessor timing refresh continues while successor timing is opening and
collecting its first observation. The native successor owns its immutable opening
baseline, so refreshing the predecessor does not replace that baseline. Freeze
predecessor probing only at the existing final G/commit boundary, where its active
role can change. Pending successor work cannot suspend ordinary source freshness
or extend an old observation's original expiry.

After owned work and cleanup are conclusively settled, an explicit Inspect may
archive the original lifecycle identities and inspect a fresh source. The archive
is bounded and never evicts unresolved custody. Inspection/reset reserves no new
effect identity; subsequent Prepare and Play still require explicit actions. Prior
history cannot reconstruct a native owner or resume a stream, and delayed results
from an archived lifecycle cannot modify its successor.

Device credentials, first-party session credentials and owner proof remain native.
The page receives bounded, redacted state and retains only its existing local
page/player capabilities, which do not become device authority. Configuration
contains exact nonsecret references and limits, not secrets or acceptance. UI
readiness is an observation; the existing Engine qualification checks remain the
last admission boundary. Pending, Synthetic or historical records are not promoted
to production acceptance by the UI.

The [isolated implementation handoff](../../docs/evidence/roadmap-2026-10-23/hsp-continuity/product-ui-next/implementation/README.md)
records formatting and mock-DOM JavaScript checks and unexecuted native Rust tests.
Canonical UI integration, compiled native tests and composed rendered HSP controls
are not established by this proposal. Current continuity qualification, useful
model/source acceptance, physical Handy response/stopping and release obligations
remain separate. This extends [B5-061](ADR-B5-061-browser-hsp-live-source.md) and
[B5-064](ADR-B5-064-continuous-source-and-clock-ownership.md) without replacing their
source or clock authority contracts.
