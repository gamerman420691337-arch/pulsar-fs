# ADR B5-030 — Bind explicit operative settings without upgrading old evidence

Qualification subjects version 2 include the complete declared slider settings and current
stroke limits. Version 1 remains readable with its original canonical identity, receipts and
withdrawal semantics, but cannot receive new acceptance or supply setup matching. Missing
settings cannot be inferred from an old evidence label or a device model. This preserves
history without silently extending what the operator reviewed. The preceding qualification-aware reader rejects the new
subject before startup mutations; the host-local SQL layout is unchanged.

Fresh read-only observations collect both settings endpoints and bracket them across
qualification acceptance and setup inspection. Exact settings equality is a configuration
condition, not proof of physical motion or stopping. Follow the schema's required
`x_limit_stop` field; the contradictory `x_limit_end` example is not treated as an alias.
No settings-write operation is added.
